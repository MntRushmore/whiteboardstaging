> Each goes through the production prompt and the route's reading of the reply (`cleanChatActions`: an invalid action is dropped). Then as the board does it: every problem verified by the engine (`verifyProblem`: it reads, is not false, `localSolve` answers it, the hand can write it); every figure checked by the drawer (`checkFigure`), given the route's one repair round-trip when it has problems, and drawn (`planFigure`); every graph's relations graphed by the engine (`graphFor`).
> **Intent** is the request's own expectations: the action types it needs, the number of problems asked for, a window when a range was asked for, no answer written for "solve it for me", no action for a request that is not maths help. **Latency** and **cost** are per request, the figure repair included.

**Pick: `openai/gpt-5.4-mini` as the chat model, with `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` as its fallback** (`LIVE_MODELS.chat` / `chatFallback`; `LIVE_MODEL_CHAT` overrides the primary). The brief's default pair, confirmed.

- GPT-5.4 mini does every request right (38/38 intent), and all 70 of its problems are verified by the engine with clean answers. All 5 figure specs pass the schema; 3 are clean at once, and the other 2 (an isosceles apex drawn 69° for 40°, a 70° angle drawn 63°) are clean after the route's one repair. It is the best US model here, as the owner wants for the primary: 2.3 s p50, 6.6 s p95 (the slow tail is the figure repair: the parallel-lines figure took 10 s plus a 7.7 s repair), about $0.0011 a request, well under the 3 credits charged.
- DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is as accurate (38/38, 70/70), faster (0.8 s p50), a third of the price, and its 5 figures were all clean the first time. On these numbers it would be the better primary; it stays the fallback only because the primary must be a US provider. It is from another provider than the primary, which is what a fallback is for.
- Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite and 3.5 Flash Lite each broke the JSON twice (text after the object; a reply cut off mid-figure), and 3.1 left a 70° angle drawn 135° after the repair. GPT-5.4 nano put invalid values in two figure specs (dropped by the route) and wrote two problems the engine cannot solve (`a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}` as a problem, `6x^{2}-5x-2`).

What the corpus does not cover: long conversations, requests in other languages, a screen crowded with the student's work, and figures more complicated than the five here (a polygon with many marks, circles with chords and tangents). The problems are judged by the engine (it reads them, `localSolve` answers them, the answers are whole numbers or simple fractions), not by a teacher for difficulty.
