> Generated by `npm run eval:chat` (src/__eval__/chat/chat.test.ts; real model calls, gated by RUN_CHAT_EVAL=1) on 2026-09-28; OpenRouter catalog of 2026-09-28.

> Each goes through the production prompt and the route's reading of the reply (`cleanChatActions`: an invalid action is dropped). Then as the board does it: every problem verified by the engine (`verifyProblem`: it reads, is not false, `localSolve` answers it, the hand can write it); every figure checked by the drawer (`checkFigure`), given the route's one repair round-trip when it has problems, and drawn (`planFigure`); every graph's relations graphed by the engine (`graphFor`).
> **Intent** is the request's own expectations: the action types it needs, the number of problems asked for, a window when a range was asked for, no answer written for "solve it for me", no action for a request that is not maths help, a proof (never a question back) written whole or set up as asked and proved by the engine, an algebra proof's every step checked. **Latency** and **cost** are per request, the figure's and the proof's repair included.

- **Proofs.** No request for a proof gets a question back any more (before: "write a proof" → "What proof do you want me to write?"), and every one of the 18 geometry proofs reached the board, the planner's rows, every one ticked by the checker. GPT-5.4 mini passed 5 at once and the other 4 after the route's one repair (2–3 s each): two right-angle / straight-angle marks the drawer refused, a triangle congruence its givens did not reach, a midpoint drawn off the middle; its "a hard proof" came back a 4-row proof after that repair, which the gate keeps (a proved short proof beats none). Both wrote both algebra proofs as `=` chains, every step verified.
- Earlier runs of this prompt found what is now fixed: GPT-5.4 mini set "write a proof" up for the student (`worked: false`) — the prompt now says the tutor writes every row unless the student asks for one to do; its "hardest" was a 5–7 row proof — a hard ask now prefers at least 8 rows, uses the proved example, and the repair can see the examples; DeepSeek's 12-row "hardest" was dropped by an 11-row cap — the board now takes 12; a point's `label` sent as a string dropped a whole figure — the route lets it go.
- Otherwise: both 49/49, all 70 problems verified with clean answers, all 5 figures drawn (GPT-5.4 mini 4 clean at once, 5 after the repair). GPT-5.4 mini 2.2 s p50, 5.6 s p95 (the slowest was "a hard proof": 10 s plus an 11 s repair); DeepSeek 1.2 s p50, 6.7 s p95.

What the corpus does not cover: long conversations, requests in other languages, a screen crowded with the student's work, figures more complicated than the five here, and proofs beyond the planner's congruent-triangle reasons (similarity, circles, addition postulates: asked for, the model is told to stay inside them). The problems are judged by the engine, not by a teacher for difficulty.
