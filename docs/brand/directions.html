Icons

2.6 pt line icons, always sitting in a solid circle: the ring of the mark. The one in use fills with ink.

Motion

Snap. Fast and sure: 150 to 250 ms, one small overshoot, then still. The tutor's tick draws in one stroke and lands with a click.

Character

No mascot. The ring, the bar and the dot get eyes only for big moments: a finished set, a new streak, a skill mastered. Older kids get a tool; younger kids still get a wink.

Our pick

A, Blocks, with C's sticker book for practice.

Blocks covers the whole K to 8 range without looking babyish to a 12 year old, and it reads as a serious tool to the parent paying $25 a month. It keeps the tutor blue that kids already know from the web app, and its whole look comes from the mark we already have.

It is also the fastest to build well in SwiftUI: flat shapes, one icon set, two font families, no illustration pipeline.

What we would borrow and watch:

  • Borrow C's reward: today's stars become stickers that fill a sticker book. It gives Blocks the collecting pull it lacks on its own.
  • Lean on motion and sound. Snap and the marimba ticks are where Blocks gets its fun, so they ship with the first build, not later.
  • Test the yellow Help me and the ink dock with two or three kids under 7 before locking them.
  • If you want it cuter, B is the safer second choice. Pip could even live inside Blocks as the tutor's face.
